You caved... It means your sanity finally perished, could no longer dominate the thousand voices screaming in your head... Begging for life, begging to be reborn, begging... for mercy...

We are merciful, after all, and could extend you a courtesy... We listened to your painful cries, tears drowned in despair. Lament no more, my beautiful naive sorcerer, your soul is already bound to this book, bound to us... Reach out for us... fight for us, and we shall aid you in reaching your zenith, or perish.

## What you are getting into

Sacrifice is a card game that is easier to learn than it looks, and where strategy (and a good dose of deception) wins far more often than luck. If you are brave enough to face it, dive into the rules and come out the newest champion of Sacrifice (though I hope you don't, so you don't take my place).

**Good luck, sorcerer!!!**
