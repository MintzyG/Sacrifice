---
title: Turns
description: Turns belong to the hand, not the table.
---

A turn belongs to the **hand**: every play or action your hand makes is one turn for you and your hand.

This matters for effects that last a number of turns, like **Stasis**. Turns are counted per hand, not once around the table, so a hand can spend several turns while other players haven't had theirs. And since effects stay on the hand, so does their turn count (see [Effect Transfer](effect-transfer)).

<Callout title="Example">
P2 (you) is blocked by **Stasis**.

1. P1 plays a Choose Color.
2. You spend your turn blocked.
3. P3 plays a Reverse.
4. You spend another turn blocked.

You've had two turns, even though the other players haven't all had theirs.
</Callout>
