---
title: Chains
description: Stacking + cards, and who ends up drawing.
---

A **Chain** is a run of **+** cards played one after another. Each one adds to the total.

- Any + card can be played on any other + card, whatever their colours. A red +4 can be played on top of a green +2.
- When a Chain reaches you, you either continue it with another + card, or draw the total and the Chain ends.

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays a green +2. The Chain is at 2.
2. P2 plays a red +4. The Chain is at 6.
3. P3 has no + card, so they draw 6. The Chain ends.
</Callout>

Some runes can be played into a Chain, like **Prosperity**, which doubles it. Each rune's page says whether it can.
