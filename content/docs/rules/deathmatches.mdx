---
title: Deathmatches
description: Drought, Dominion and The Wrath.
---

A Deathmatch is an event where no player can draw, and anyone who draws dies. There are three: **Drought**, **Dominion** and **The Wrath**.

In any Deathmatch, **Tainted** players can choose not to draw.

## Drought

**Starts** when the draw pile runs out, so there is nothing left to draw from.

**Still allowed:**
- **Servants** can still draw from their **Master**.
- Swapping hands with **Soul Swap**.
- Stealing cards with **Draining**.
- Killing with **Soul Taker**.
- Using **Healing**.

## Dominion

**Starts** when the **Dominion** rune is played. Every player's hand rotates to the next player, in the direction of play. From then on, drawing in any way kills you.

**Allowed without dying:**
- Using **Healing**.
- **Servants** drawing from their **Master**.
- **Starved** players drawing.
- Stealing cards with **Draining**.

**Kills everyone involved:**
- Swapping hands with **Soul Swap**.
- Killing with **Soul Taker**.

## The Wrath

**Starts** when the game timer runs out, after 10 minutes.

The sorcerers began summoning the demon from the first card, and left it stuck halfway, not quite summoned. Now it wants to end them all.

Getting a card in any way kills you. The only exception is reviving with **Miracle**: it gives you a card, and you don't die.
