---
title: Hail Mary
description: One last turn to stop a win.
---

When a player wins, the next player still gets one turn. In that turn they can do whatever they want about the win: stop it, or let it happen. If they can't do anything either way, the player wins.

They can even use the turn to protect the win, for example by throwing in a random card so nobody can play **Impermanence** on it.

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 wins.
2. P2 plays a green 3.
3. P3 plays Impermanence.
4. Impermanence cancels the green 3, not the victory. P1 wins.
</Callout>
