title: Basic Rules
description: Setting up, playing, dying and winning.
---

## Setup

1. Each player starts with 7 cards dealt from the deck.
2. A random player shuffles the demon deck, draws one demon and places it face down on the table, then turns it over to reveal its colour.
3. Anyone except the player who revealed the demon may make the first play, with a card of that colour.
4. Then the two players next to whoever made the first play must play on it. Whichever of them plays a valid card first takes the play, and the game continues normally from there. If the player who revealed the demon is one of those two players, they may take part in this second play.

## Playing

1. You may cheat, as long as it isn't against the manual.
2. Hiding cards is forbidden.
3. Whenever a card lets you choose a colour and you choose it before the next player plays, the next player must play a card of that colour or a purple card; otherwise the play is invalid. The exception is **+** cards: if the next card is a **+** card, the chosen colour doesn't apply.
4. A game lasts 10 minutes; after that, [The Wrath](./deathmatches.mdx#the-wrath) begins.

## Dying

1. When you die, your hand still matters. Don't mix it with other cards; keep it separate.
2. If drawing would kill you, draw the cards first, then die.

## Winning

1. The game ends when:
   - a player completes their [Sacrifice](./sacrifice.mdx) and no event in play stops them from winning,
   - only one player is left alive, or
   - no player is left alive, in which case nobody wins.
2. Exactly one player wins, or nobody does. There are no ties.
3. If the winner isn't clear at the end of the game, the players, or someone from outside, must review how it ended.
4. Declaring victory is forbidden.
5. Preventing the verification of a win is forbidden.
6. Mixing your hand with other cards is forbidden until the winner has been announced.
