---
title: Piles
description: Where cards go once they leave your hand.
---

## Graveyard

Every discarded card goes to the graveyard, and so does the hand of every player who dies, unless their cards were passed to other players.

No player can access the graveyard. The only way in is **Restoration**, which lets a player choose 3 cards to draw from it.

<Callout title="Example">
**Crushing** discards 3 cards from your hand. Those cards go to the graveyard.
</Callout>

## Used pile

Every card that is played goes to the used pile, in the order it was played, with the most recent on top.

The used pile is the game's timeline. Judges can use it to review plays when needed, and the game uses it too, for example to revive a player during a Deathmatch when there is no draw pile left.

Some runes interact with the used pile, undoing plays or taking cards from it: **Recollection**, **Time** and **Healing**.
