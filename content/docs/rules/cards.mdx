## Colours

The deck has six colours: **red**, **yellow**, **green**, **blue**, **black** and **white**.

**Purple** is the God's colour, outside the six. Purple cards are imbued with the God's magic: the basic purple cards below, and the [runes](../runes/how-runes-work.mdx).

## Number cards

Each colour has the numbers **0** to **9**, plus **Triquetra** and **Infinity**. Triquetra and Infinity are ordinary numbers, but they can't be part of a [Sequence](./rainbow-and-sequence.mdx#sequence).

## Coloured action cards

Each colour also has these, which work as in Uno:

- **+2** and **+4:** the next player draws 2 or 4, or continues the [Chain](./chains.mdx). Inside a Chain, colours don't matter.
- **Reverse:** the direction of play reverses. With only 2 players, it acts as a Block.
- **Block:** the next player is skipped.

## Purple cards

Every purple card is wild: it can be played on top of anything.

Whoever plays a purple card may choose a colour, but only before the next player plays. If they choose, the next player must play the same symbol, that colour, or a purple card. If the next player plays first, the chance to choose is gone, and their play stands.

Like runes, the basic purple cards have a **Tainted** version, when you're [Tainted](../runes/tainted.mdx), and a **Transmutation** version, when played together with [Transmutation](../runes/transmutation.mdx).

| Card | Effect | Tainted | Transmutation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wild** | Choose a colour. | No change. | **Supreme Choose Color:** see the next player's hand and choose exactly what they do on their turn, as if you were them. |
| **+1** | +1 | +2 | Every other player draws 1. |
| **+2** | +2 | +4 | **Gift:** instead of drawing, the next player gets 2 cards of your choice from your hand. |
| **+4** | +4 | +8 | **Supreme +4:** the next player discards 4 purple cards, or ordinary cards if they don't have enough, and loses their turn. With fewer than 5 cards, they die. |
| **+20** | +20 | +40 | **Hunt the leader:** the +20 goes to the player with the fewest cards. On a tie, all of them get it. |
| **Reverse** | The direction reverses. | Doesn't reverse. | **Reflection:** the last effect played on you goes back to whoever played it, even one that already happened, like a Reaper kill you were revived from. If that player is dead, go back one more effect, until you reach one whose player is alive. |
| **Block** | The next player is skipped. | Blocks 2 players. | **Ambush:** keep it face down in front of you. Spring it at any moment, even out of turn, to block a player when their turn comes. |
| **Double Block** | The next 2 players are skipped. | Blocks 4 players. | Every other player is blocked once, so you play again. |
| **Target Block** | Choose a player. They're blocked when their turn comes. | Choose 2 players. | You take the chosen player's turn, playing from your own hand. |
| **Target +2** | Choose a player to get the +2. On a Chain, it sends the whole Chain to them, and they can continue it. | +4 | **Curse:** the chosen player draws 1 at the start of each of their next 3 turns. |
| **Initiate Transmutation** | Discard 4 cards, then draw 4. | Discard 8, draw 8. | Discard any number of cards, then draw the same number. |

**Tainted and Transmutation together:**

- **Wild:** Supreme Choose Color for the next two players.
- **+1:** every other player draws 2.
- **+2:** Gift 4 cards.
- **+4:** Supreme +4 with 8 cards. With fewer than 9, they die.
- **+20:** Hunt the leader with +40.
- **Reverse:** Reflection on the last two effects played on you.
- **Block:** two Ambushes.
- **Double Block:** every other player is blocked twice.
- **Target Block:** take two players' turns.
- **Target +2:** Curse for 6 turns.
- **Initiate Transmutation:** no change.

## The demon deck

Besides the main deck, there's a small deck of 6 **demons**, one for each colour. It's only used to start the game (see [Basic Rules](./basic-rules.mdx#setup)).
