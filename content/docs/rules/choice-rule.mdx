---
title: Choice Rule
description: Once you choose a player, there's no going back.
---

Whenever a play lets you choose a player to target, the first mention of a player locks the choice onto them. A mention is any of:

- pointing at them,
- saying half their name,
- saying "you" while looking at them.

The choice can't be taken back. And once a player has been chosen, the play is no longer covered by the [Five-Second Rule](five-second-rule).
