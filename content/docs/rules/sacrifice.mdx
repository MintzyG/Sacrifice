title: Sacrifice

Running out of cards isn't enough. To win, you have to complete a **Sacrifice**, or be the last player alive.

Every player has a **Sacrificial Dagger**: an inventory card that isn't part of the deck.

## Pledging

When you're down to your last two cards, on your turn:

1. Play your penultimate card.
2. Place your last card under your dagger, hidden. This is your **pledge**: you've chosen your last play one turn in advance.

Everyone keeps playing normally, trying to stop it.

## Your Sacrifice turn

On your next turn, you either reveal or postpone.

### Reveal

Reveal the pledged card and play it. If it's a valid play, you win, once all events and effects have resolved and the next player has had their [Hail Mary](./hail-mary.mdx). If it isn't, the Sacrifice fails.

### Postpone

Instead of revealing, draw one card and play it immediately. Your pledge stays under the dagger. If you can't play the card you drew, the Sacrifice fails.

You can only postpone when you'd be allowed to draw. For example, if the last card played is a **+** card, you have to reveal.

A **Starved** player can never postpone. For them it's all or nothing: reveal, and either win or die.

<Callout title="Example">
You have a green 5 and a red 7.

1. You play the green 5, and pledge the red 7 under your dagger.
2. The other players play. When your turn comes, the top card is red, so you reveal the red 7. It's a valid play: after the Hail Mary, you win.
3. If someone had changed the colour to blue, the red 7 would no longer be a valid play. You could postpone and hope for red later, or reveal and fail.
</Callout>

## Failed Sacrifice

When a Sacrifice fails, you draw 7, and the pledged card goes back into your hand. You pledge again the next time you play your penultimate card.

Playing **Impermanence** on your pledge also makes the Sacrifice fail.

## Pledging a purple card

You can pledge a purple card, but it's a risk: if that Sacrifice fails, you die immediately instead of drawing 7. With a [bloody dagger](#bloody-dagger), a failed purple pledge is just a normal failure: you draw 7.

## Bloody dagger

Your dagger becomes bloody when you kill a player.

### Early Sacrifice

With a bloody dagger, you can make an **Early Sacrifice** on any of your turns, except your Sacrifice turn. Play your bloody dagger together with the card, as a normal play: the card must be a valid play. It must be one of:

- a card of the colour of the demon the game started on, as a sacrifice to that demon, or
- a purple card, as a sacrifice to the God.

No other card can be an Early Sacrifice. After it:

- you can no longer win by Sacrifice; you can only win by killing,
- you gain a bonus from that demon or from the God.
