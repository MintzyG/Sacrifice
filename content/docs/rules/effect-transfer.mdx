---
title: Effect Transfer
description: Effects stay on the hand, not the player.
---

When a player puts an effect on another, like **Famine**, the effect stays on the **hand**, not on the player. This is true for every effect.

A sorcerer's hand is their **soul**. Effects bind to the soul, not the body holding it.

So whenever hands change owners, whether swapped by **Soul Swap** or moved around by **Transference**, the effect goes with the hand, and someone else ends up with it.

<Callout title="Example">
P2 has the Famine effect.

1. P1 plays **Soul Swap** with P2.
2. The effect stays on the hand, so P1 now has the Famine effect, and P2 no longer does.
</Callout>

## Exceptions

- If **Pandemonium** is played, every effect is lost.
- If a player is killed by **Reaper**, their effects don't pass to the player who killed them.
