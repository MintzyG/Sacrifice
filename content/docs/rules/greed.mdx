---
title: Greed
description: Draw 20 or more at once, and some of the runes have to go.
---

Whenever you draw 20 or more cards at once, you've been greedy. From the cards you just drew, discard runes: one fewer than the tens digit of the number you drew. They go to the [graveyard](./piles.mdx#graveyard).

| You draw | Runes to discard |
|---|---|
| 20–29 | 1 |
| 30–39 | 2 |
| 40–49 | 3 |

And so on: every 10 more cards, one more rune.

This counts for any draw of 20 or more, whatever caused it. You choose which runes to discard.

## Not enough runes

If the cards you drew don't have enough runes, discard runes from the rest of your hand until you've paid the full amount.

If you still don't have enough, you're **Indebted**: every rune you get from then on must be discarded, until the debt is paid.

The debt stays on the hand, like any effect (see [Effect Transfer](./effect-transfer.mdx)). [Purity](../runes/purity.mdx) clears it.

<Callout title="Example">
A [Chain](./chains.mdx) of 32 reaches P3, who can't continue it. P3 draws 32, then discards 2 of the runes among them.
</Callout>
