title: The Rite

**The Rite** is the order in which everything in a turn resolves. It's used to analyse and reproduce games: when a play causes several things at once, resolve them step by step, in this order.

## The steps

1. Card play
2. Happening
3. Event
4. Effect
5. Death
6. Victory
7. End of turn

A turn starts at **Card play** and ends at **End of turn**.

## What each step means

- **Card play:** the card, or cards, are played.
- **Happening:** something triggered by the game itself, not directly by a player's choice.
- **Event:** something that changes the table as a whole: the game state, every player or every hand. For example, every hand rotating with **Transference**, **Pandemonium**, or a [Deathmatch](./deathmatches.mdx) starting.
- **Effect:** something that lands on specific hands, like becoming **Starved**, becoming a **Master** or **Servant**, or being blocked by **Stasis**. Effects stay on the hand (see [Effect Transfer](./effect-transfer.mdx)).
- **Death:** players who die this turn die.
- **Victory:** wins are checked.
- **End of turn:** the turn is over.

## Winning during a Chain

If your revealed [pledge](./sacrifice.mdx) continues a [Chain](./chains.mdx), you only win once the Chain is over. The Chain might come back around to you, and then you have to draw.
