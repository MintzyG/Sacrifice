---
title: Five-Second Rule
description: How long you have to take back a play.
---

After a play, you have 5 seconds to take it back and redo it. After that, the play can no longer be changed.

If the next player plays before the 5 seconds are up, your play can no longer be undone.

## Choosing a colour

When you play a card that lets you choose the colour for the next play (like a **Choose Color**), you may only choose the colour before the next player makes their play.

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays a **Choose Color**, planning to choose red.
2. P2 plays a green 4 before P1 chooses red.
3. P1 can no longer choose the colour, so P2's play is valid.
</Callout>

## Choice cards

Several cards that make you choose something, like **Impotence**, **Famine** and **Soul Swap**, block the next play until the choice has been made.

Once a player has been chosen as a target, the play can no longer be taken back. See the [Choice Rule](choice-rule).
