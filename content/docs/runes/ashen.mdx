---
title: Ashen
description: Make a player Colourblind. Colours stop mattering to them.
---

## Effect

Choose a living player, yourself included. They become **Colourblind**: the colour rules don't apply to them. They can play any card on any card, whatever the colour on top, or the colour someone chose. Only the colour rule is gone: every other rule still applies, like having to continue a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx) with a + card or draw. The choice follows the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

Being Colourblind stays on the hand (see [Effect Transfer](../rules/effect-transfer.mdx)).

<Callout title="Beware">
An [Impotent](./impotence.mdx) player who is Colourblind has to follow the colour rules, even for purple cards. Their purple cards aren't wild anymore.
</Callout>

Ashen can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

<Rite

Choose two players.

## Transmutation

Choose three players.
