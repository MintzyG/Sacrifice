title: Fortune

## Effect

Fortune can be played in two ways:

- **On a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx):** choose any living player. They draw all of it.
- **On its own:** it works as a Choose Color.

The choice follows the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

## The Rite

<Rite
  play="Fortune is played on a Chain, and a player is chosen."
  effect="The chosen player draws all of it."
/>

## Tainted

The chosen player draws double.

## Transmutation

The chosen player draws triple.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** the chosen player draws six times the Chain.

## Exceptions

- **[Protection](./protection.mdx) can't block Fortune.**
- **Fortune can block Protection.** If someone uses Protection to send a Chain to you, you can play Fortune on it, and still choose anyone to draw.
- **A Shattered Impermanence can.** If you're the one chosen to draw, you can use a Shattered Impermanence to protect yourself.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays a +2. P2 plays a +4. The Chain is at 6.
2. P3 plays Protection, sending it to P1.
3. P1 plays Fortune on it, and chooses P4. P4 draws 6.
</Callout>
