---
title: Impotence
description: Make a player Impotent. Their purple cards lose all their power.
---

Purple cards are imbued by the God: their power is magic. An Impotent player has lost the ability to use and control it.

## Effect

Choose a living player, yourself included. They become **Impotent**. The choice follows the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

For an Impotent player, every purple card in their hand is a common purple wild card: a plain Choose Color. That's every purple card, runes and purple +2, +4, Reverse and Block alike, including the ones they get later.

- **Purity** and [Miracle](./miracle.mdx) are the exceptions. Purity's power is far greater: it gives power even to the powerless. Miracle is the God's own blessing, bound to your essence.
- The change only applies to the Impotent player. If someone else gets one of their cards, it works normally.
- Becoming Impotent again changes nothing: you stay Impotent.
- Being Impotent stays on the hand (see [Effect Transfer](../rules/effect-transfer.mdx)).

Impotence can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## Tainted

Choose two players.

An Impotent player's [Tainted](./tainted.mdx) becomes a common purple wild card too, so they're no longer Tainted.

## Transmutation

The chosen player becomes allergic to magic: they discard every purple card in their hand, and keep discarding any purple card they get, until they die or the game ends.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** choose two players.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays Impotence on P2, who is holding Stasis and a purple +4.
2. For P2, both are now plain Choose Colors.
3. Later, P3 steals the Stasis with Draining. For P3, it's Stasis again.
</Callout>
