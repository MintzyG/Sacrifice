## Effect

Every hand passes to the next player, in the direction of play. Then [The Transference](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-transference) Deathmatch begins.

Dead players' hands rotate too. A dead hand has no cards, but it passes the death on:

- A living player who receives a dead hand dies.
- A dead player who receives a living hand comes back alive, with those cards.
- A dead player who receives a dead hand stays dead.

Effects go with the hands (see [Effect Transfer](../rules/effect-transfer.mdx)).

Transference can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## Tainted

The hands move 2 players along.

## Transmutation

The hands move 3 players along.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** the hands move 6 players along.

## Deathmatches

- **[The Drought](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-drought):** nothing special.
- **[The Transference](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-transference):** playing it again cancels the Deathmatch. The hands still rotate once more.
- **[The Wrath](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-wrath):** everyone dies.

## Exceptions

- **Your last card:** if Transference is your last card, the player who receives your hand wins. The demon recognises the soul being transferred.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
P1, P2 and P3 are alive. P4 is dead. Play goes P1 → P2 → P3 → P4.

1. P2 plays Transference.
2. P1's hand goes to P2, P2's to P3, P3's to P4, and P4's dead hand to P1.
3. P1 dies. P4 comes back alive, with P3's old hand.
</Callout>
