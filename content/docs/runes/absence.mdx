---
title: Absence
description: Send a player into the shadow world for 3 turns.
---

## Effect

On your turn, choose a player, yourself included. They become **Absent** for their next 3 turns: they're in the shadow world, off the game. The choice follows the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

While Absent, a player:

- can't be targeted by anything,
- doesn't count as a player,
- can't be made to draw, so they can't die from drawing, even in a [Deathmatch](../rules/deathmatches.mdx).

When their 3 turns are over, they come back, and play normally.

<Callout title="Beware">
An Absent player doesn't count as a player, even for winning. With 3 players, if one is Absent and another dies, only one player is left, and they win.
</Callout>

## Escaping a Chain

When a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx) reaches you, you can play Absence on yourself to escape it. The Chain skips you.

## Tainted

The chosen player is Absent for 6 turns.

## Transmutation

**Nightfall:** nobody becomes Absent. Instead, you get a counter equal to the number of players, you included, plus 3. Each of your turns, it goes down by one. If you're still alive when it runs out, night falls: from then on, on each of your turns, a player of your choice dies. It goes on until you die, or you win.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** Nightfall, but two players of your choice die on each of your turns.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
There are 5 players, so P1's Nightfall counter starts at 8.

1. P1 plays Absence with Transmutation.
2. Eight of P1's turns later, P1 is still alive. Night falls.
3. From then on, on each of P1's turns, P1 chooses a player, and they die.
</Callout>
