---
title: Soul Swap
description: Swap hands with another player.
---

## Effect

Choose another living player, and swap hands with them. You can't choose yourself, or a dead player. The choice follows the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

Any effects on the two hands go with them (see [Effect Transfer](../rules/effect-transfer.mdx)).

Soul Swap can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## The Rite

<Rite
  play="Soul Swap is played, and a player is chosen."
  event="You and the chosen player swap hands."
/>

## Tainted

No change.

## Transmutation

You swap hands, but not effects: each player keeps their own effects.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** the same, you swap hands but not effects.

## Deathmatches

- **[Drought](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#drought):** nothing special.
- **[Dominion](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#dominion)** and **[The Wrath](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-wrath):** both players die. An **Impermanence** or a **Shattered Impermanence** can stop the swap.

## Exceptions

- **Your last card:** if Soul Swap is your last card, you don't have to swap. It works as a Choose Color instead.
- **Miracle:** it stays with its original owner. It doesn't move with the hand.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
P2 is Starved, and has 3 cards. P1 has 9.

1. P1 plays Soul Swap and points at P2.
2. P1 now has P2's 3 cards, and is Starved. P2 has P1's 9 cards.
</Callout>
