---
title: Reaper
description: Kill a player, and take their hand.
---

## Effect

Choose another living player. They die, and you take every card in their hand. If you play Reaper, you must kill someone. The choice follows the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

- Their effects don't pass to you (see [Effect Transfer](../rules/effect-transfer.mdx)).
- If they're [Tainted](./tainted.mdx), Tainted comes to you with the rest of their cards.
- If they hold [Miracle](./miracle.mdx), it stays with them, and revives them with a new hand.

You can't choose yourself.

Reaper can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## The Rite

<Rite
  play="Reaper is played, and a player is chosen."
  effect="You take every card in the chosen player's hand."
  death="The chosen player dies."
/>

## Gluttony

A [Starved](./famine.mdx) Reaper commits **Gluttony**: taking the hand counts as drawing, so they die too. During [The Transference](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-transference), they don't.

## Tainted

Kill two players. You can choose the same player twice: if they hold Miracle, the second kill is for good.

## Transmutation

Kill three players.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** kill six players.

## Deathmatches

- **[The Drought](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-drought):** works normally.
- **[The Transference](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-transference)** and **[The Wrath](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-wrath):** both you and the player you chose die.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays Reaper on P3, who has 5 cards and is Starved.
2. P3 dies. P1 takes their 5 cards, but P1 doesn't become Starved.
</Callout>
