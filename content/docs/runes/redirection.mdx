---
title: Redirection
description: Make a Scapegoat draw for a Sinner.
---

## Effect

Choose a **Scapegoat** and a **Sinner**. One player can't be both in the same play, but you can choose yourself for either role. The choices follow the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

Whenever the Sinner has to draw because of someone else's play, like a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx), a **Protection** or a **Fortune**, they can send the Scapegoat to draw the cards for them. The Sinner can still choose to draw them themselves.

Draws the Sinner causes themselves can't be redirected: drawing because they have nothing to play, or the 7 cards from a failed [Sacrifice](../rules/sacrifice.mdx).

The roles stay on the hand (see [Effect Transfer](../rules/effect-transfer.mdx)).

Redirection can't be played into a Chain.

## More than one role

- With more than one Scapegoat, the Sinner chooses which one draws.
- You can be a Sinner more than once, with different Scapegoats.
- You can be a Scapegoat and a Sinner at the same time, as long as it's for different players. For example: P1 is P2's Sinner, and P2 is also P3's Sinner.

## Tainted

Choose two Scapegoats and two Sinners.

## Transmutation

Choose a Sinner and a **Super Scapegoat**. The first time a draw for the Sinner would kill the Super Scapegoat, they survive it.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** the Super Scapegoat survives twice.

## Deathmatches

When the Scapegoat draws for the Sinner, it counts as a normal draw for the Scapegoat. If it would kill them, they die, and the Sinner loses their Scapegoat. If it wouldn't, they don't: for example, a Scapegoat who is also a **Servant** can still draw from their Master during [The Drought](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-drought) without dying.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays Redirection: P2 is the Scapegoat, P1 is the Sinner.
2. Later, a Chain of 8 reaches P1, who has no + card.
3. P1 sends P2 to draw. P2 draws 8, and play continues.
</Callout>
