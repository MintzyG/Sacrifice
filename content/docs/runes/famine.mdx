title: Famine
description: Make a player Starved. They can't draw, or they die.
---

## Effect

Choose a living player, yourself included. They become **Starved**: they can no longer draw, and if they draw, they die. The choice follows the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

If the chosen player is already Starved, they starve: they die.

Being Starved stays on the hand (see [Effect Transfer](../rules/effect-transfer.mdx)).

Famine can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx). A Starved player who has to draw a Chain draws it, then dies.

## Starved with other roles

- **Servant:** a Starved [Servant](./empowering.mdx) draws from their Master, then dies. The Master no longer has that Servant.
- **Master:** a Starved Master is still a Master. They can't draw, but their Servants still draw from them.
- **Tainted:** a Starved player who is also Tainted can still choose not to draw in any [Deathmatch](../rules/deathmatches.mdx).
- **Scapegoat:** a Starved [Scapegoat](./redirection.mdx) who draws for their Sinner dies.

## Tainted

Choose two players.

## Transmutation

The game is under **Rationing** for the rest of the game. Every draw is dealt out one card at a time: the first card to the player who draws, the next to the player after them, and so on around the table, until the cards run out.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** Rationing, but every draw is halved, rounding up, before it's dealt out. A draw of 3 becomes 2.

## Deathmatches

- **[The Transference](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-transference):** Starved players can draw without dying.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
The game is under Rationing, with 5 players.

2. P2 takes the first card, P3 the second, P4 the third. P5 and P1 get nothing.
