## Effect

Repeat the effect of the last purple card on the [used pile](../rules/piles.mdx#used-pile), as if you had played it. Any purple card counts: runes, and purple +2, +4, Reverse, Block and Choose Color. Any choices the effect needs, like a target or a colour, are yours to make.

Recollection can be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx) only if the effect it repeats can be: a +2, a +4, **Prosperity**, **Protection**, and so on.

## God's Touch

If there's no purple card on the used pile, you get **God's Touch** instead: use the effect of any card in the game.

## Tainted

Repeat the effects of the last two purple cards, most recent first.

## Transmutation

Use the effect of a purple card in your hand, without spending it.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** use the effects of two purple cards in your hand, without spending them.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays Vision on P2. P2 shows their hand.
2. P2 plays a red 4.
3. P3 plays Recollection. Vision is the last purple card, so P3 repeats it and chooses P1, who shows their hand.
</Callout>
