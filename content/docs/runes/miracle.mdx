title: Miracle
description: The God's blessing. When you die, you come back.
---

Miracle is a blessing from the God of the game itself. It's bound to your essence, which is more than your soul, so it doesn't leave you when your hand does.

## Bound to you

Once Miracle is in your hand, other players can't take it from you. When hands move, by [Soul Swap](./soul-swap.mdx), [Transference](./transference.mdx), [Draining](./draining.mdx), a [Servant](./empowering.mdx) drawing, or [Pandemonium](./pandemonium.mdx), Miracle stays with you.

The one exception: another player can destroy it with **Crushing** played with Transmutation.

Being [Impotent](./impotence.mdx) doesn't affect Miracle: it keeps its power.

## Effect

**When you die,** Miracle always activates by itself and revives you.

**On your turn,** you can play it to revive another player, as long as someone is dead. Choose a dead player, and they come back.

Either way, the revived player gets a new hand: 7 cards, or 1 during any [Deathmatch](../rules/deathmatches.mdx). The cards come from the draw pile, or, if there's no draw pile left, at random from the [used pile](../rules/piles.mdx#used-pile).

Once it has revived someone, Miracle goes to the used pile like any played card. From there, [Healing](./healing.mdx) can take it back, and [Recollection](./recollection.mdx) can repeat it.

Miracle can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## Pledging Miracle

You can pledge Miracle as your last card. It's purple, so if the [Sacrifice](../rules/sacrifice.mdx) fails, you die. And then Miracle revives you.

If nobody is dead when you reveal it, Miracle works as a Choose Color.

## Tainted

No change.

## Transmutation

Miracle is duplicated. You keep both.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** the same.
