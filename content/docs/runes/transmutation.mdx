## Effect

Play Transmutation together with another purple card. That card uses its **Transmutation** version, described on its own page. Both cards are spent, with Transmutation on top.

Played alone, Transmutation is just a Choose Color. You can also pledge it as your last card: it's a Choose Color then too.

Transmutation can be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx) only if the card it's paired with can be.

## With basic purple cards

Every basic purple card has a Transmutation version too, like the **Supreme Choose Color** from a Wild, or the **Ambush** from a Block. They're all listed on the [Cards](../rules/cards.mdx#purple-cards) page.

## Tainted

No change.
