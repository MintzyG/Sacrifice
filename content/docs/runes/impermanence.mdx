---
title: Impermanence
description: Cancel anything, at any moment.
---

## Effect

Impermanence cancels one step of [The Rite](../rules/the-rite.mdx): something that has just happened.

- **Any moment:** you can play it on your turn or out of turn. Out of turn, it doesn't use up your turn, and play continues from whoever was due to play next. On your turn, it counts as your play.
- **Only after:** it can only be played once something has actually happened. You can't play it ahead of time.
- **Cards back:** it only returns a card to someone's hand if that card was a number, or a winning pledge.

Impermanence can be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx), to stop it.

## Special cases

Whatever Impermanence cancels simply doesn't happen. These are the cases where something more happens:

| Cancelled | What happens |
|---|---|
| A [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx) | It stops. The next player can play anything. |
| The deal | The cards are shuffled and dealt again. |
| A number card | It goes back to its player, who loses that play. |
| A colour choice | You choose the colour instead. |
| A death from drawing in a [Deathmatch](../rules/deathmatches.mdx) | The player is skipped instead, so they don't die. |
| A death from a + card | They don't die, but they still draw. If they can't, play moves on. |
| [Reaper](./reaper.mdx) | The Reaper is cancelled, and you get to use it yourself, right away. |
| [Tainted](./tainted.mdx) | Both cards are used up, and Tainted is gone. |
| A [pledge](../rules/sacrifice.mdx) | The Sacrifice fails: draw 7. |
| A revealed, winning pledge | The player takes the card and their dagger back. They have to pledge again, and win again. |

## Exceptions

- Impermanence cancels things that happen, not cards. You can't play it on someone's hand to wipe it out.
- While you're blocked by [Stasis](./stasis.mdx), you can't use Impermanence.
- A Shattered Impermanence (from [Protection](./protection.mdx)) can't block Impermanence.

## Tainted

No change.

## Transmutation

Impermanence is duplicated. One copy is used right away, and you keep the other for later.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** the same.

<Callout title="Doomsday">
With Impermanence duplicated by Transmutation, and Transmutation repeated by [Recollection](./recollection.mdx), the same plays can cancel each other back and forth forever. There's no rule to stop it. It's rare, but if it happens, it happens: that's **Doomsday**.
</Callout>
