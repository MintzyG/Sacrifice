title: Echo
description: Mirror a purple card. Use it twice now, or keep the original.
---

## Effect

Play Echo together with any purple card. Echo mirrors it, and you choose:

- **Twice now:** play both. The card's effect happens twice, as if you'd played two of it.
- **Keep it:** play only Echo. It mirrors the card's effect, and the original card stays in your hand, for later.

Any choices the effect needs are yours to make, each time it happens.

Echo can be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx) only if the card it mirrors can be. In a [Deathmatch](../rules/deathmatches.mdx), it works like the card it mirrors.

## Tainted

The mirrored effect is the card's Tainted version.

## Transmutation

The mirrored effect happens 2 more times.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** the Tainted version, 2 more times.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays Echo with Vision, and chooses to keep it.
2. Echo works as Vision: P3 shows their hand.
3. P1 still has Vision in their hand, for later.
</Callout>
