title: Displacement
description: Two players swap seats, and take over the hand they find there.

## Effect

Choose two different players, yourself included. They swap seats at the table, but the hands stay where they are: each player takes over the hand in their new seat, with every effect on it. It's a change of body, not of soul: the souls stay put, and the players move between them. The choices follow the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

- **[Miracle](./miracle.mdx)** and **[Tainted](./tainted.mdx)** stay with the hand. Your essence is split, and Miracle stays with the greater part: your soul.
- **[Deathmatches](../rules/deathmatches.mdx):** unlike [Soul Swap](./soul-swap.mdx), a Displacement never kills anyone, in any Deathmatch.

Displacement can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## Tainted

Choose three players, and decide who moves to whose seat.

## Transmutation

**Singularity:** the two chosen players' souls merge: two bodies, one soul. Their hands become one. They're still two different players, each taking their own turns, but both play from the same hand. What happens to one happens to the other, even a win: if their merged soul wins, they both win. It's the one exception to only one player winning.

Whichever of them gets to pledge first, pledges. And if you don't want the other one to win, you can pledge a card you know will fail, and draw 7 on purpose.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** a Singularity of three players.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
P3 has 2 cards left, and is about to pledge. P1 has 12.

1. P1 plays Displacement on themselves and P3.
2. P1 and P3 swap seats. P1 now plays the 2-card hand, and P3 is stuck with the 12.
</Callout>
