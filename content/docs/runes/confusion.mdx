## Effect

Choose a living player, yourself included. They become **Confused**: every rune in their hand counts as the **next rune in this manual**, in the order of the sidebar. The last rune wraps around to the first. The choice follows the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

It only applies to runes, but to all of them, including [Tainted](./tainted.mdx) and [Transmutation](./transmutation.mdx). A Confused player holding Tainted isn't Tainted: their Tainted counts as the next rune. Their other cards, purple or not, work normally.

So to play any rune while Confused, you need the rune just before it. To transmute, for example, you need a [Reaper](./reaper.mdx): it counts as Transmutation.

Being Confused stays on the hand (see [Effect Transfer](../rules/effect-transfer.mdx)).

Confusion can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## Tainted

Choose two players.

## Transmutation

The chosen player's runes have no fixed identity anymore. Each time they play one, it becomes a random rune: number the runes in the order of this manual, and roll a die over that many to decide which.

The rune is only decided when it's played. If the roll lands on Tainted, that card becomes Tainted, and they're Tainted.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** choose two players.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays Confusion on P2.
2. P2 plays Vision. The next rune in the manual is Protection, so it works as Protection.
</Callout>
