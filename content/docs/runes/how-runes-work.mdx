---
title: How Runes Work
description: The purple cards, and how to read their pages.
---

Runes are the purple cards. They bend the game: they can target other players, affect everyone, change the rules for the rest of the game, and combine with each other.

## Playing runes

A rune can be played on top of any card. Some runes can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx); each rune's page says whether it can.

## Tainted and Transmutation

Every rune has two other versions:

- **Tainted:** what it does when you're **Tainted**.
- **Transmutation:** what it does when played together with the **Transmutation** rune.

## Reading a rune page

- **Effect:** what the rune does.
- **The Rite:** every step of [The Rite](../rules/the-rite.mdx) the rune takes part in, all seven shown, so you can see its full extent. A step marked "—" isn't affected.
- **Tainted** and **Transmutation:** how its other versions differ.
- **Deathmatches:** only when the rune works differently in a [Deathmatch](../rules/deathmatches.mdx).
- **Exceptions** and **Examples**, when there are any.
