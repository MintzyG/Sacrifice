---
title: Stasis
description: Block the next player for 2 turns, unless they pass it on.
---

## Effect

When Stasis is played, the next player chooses:

- **Take it:** they're blocked for 2 turns.
- **Pass it on:** they play a Block, and the next player faces the same choice, with 1 more turn.

It keeps going until a player can't, or won't, pass it on. That player is blocked for the total.

Any Block can pass it on: a coloured Block, a purple Block, a purple Double Block or a purple Target Block.

Stasis can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## While blocked

- The turn you take the block in doesn't count. Your blocked turns start after it.
- You can't be blocked again. A Block played on you blocks the next player instead.
- A + card aimed at you skips you: it goes to the player after you, and you have one less turn left blocked.
- You can still be chosen as a target, for example by **Reaper** or **Draining**.
- The block stays on the hand (see [Effect Transfer](../rules/effect-transfer.mdx)). If someone swaps hands with you, they're blocked for the turns you had left, and you aren't.

## Tainted

The block starts at 4 turns. A Block that passes it on adds 1 turn, or 2 if the player who plays it is Tainted.

## Transmutation

Every player except you is blocked for 3 turns.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** every player except you is blocked for 6 turns.

## Examples

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays Stasis.
2. P2 takes it, and is blocked for 2 turns.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays Stasis.
2. P2 plays a Block, passing it on.
3. P3 takes it, and is blocked for 3 turns.
</Callout>
