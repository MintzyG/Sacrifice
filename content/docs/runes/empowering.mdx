---
title: Empowering
description: Make one player the Master, and another their Servant.
---

## Effect

Choose a **Master** and a **Servant**. One player can't be both in the same play, but you can choose yourself for either role. The choices follow the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

- **Servant:** no longer draws from the draw pile. Instead, they draw from the top of the Master's hand, as if it were the draw pile.
- **Master:** can no longer reorder their hand. Cards they draw go to the back of it.

Empowering can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## The Rite

<Rite
  play="Empowering is played, and a Master and a Servant are chosen."
  effect="The chosen players become Master and Servant."
/>

## When the Servant can't draw

- If the Servant has to draw more cards than the Master can give, they draw until the Master has 1 card left. The Master then pledges that card on their turn, or draws more.
- When the Master is down to their [pledge](../rules/sacrifice.mdx), the Servant can't draw at all: they're in [The Drought](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-drought).
- **Miracle** stays wherever it is in the Master's hand, and the Servant can't draw past it. When the Servant reaches it, they're in The Drought.

## More than one role

- You can be a Master more than once, with different Servants.
- You can be a Servant more than once. When you draw, choose which Master's hand to draw from.
- You can be a Master and a Servant at the same time.
- A **Starved** Master is still a Master. They can't draw themselves, but their Servants still draw from them.

## Tainted

Choose two Masters and two Servants.

## Transmutation

Choose three Masters and three Servants.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** choose six Masters and six Servants.

## Deathmatches

- **[The Drought](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-drought)** and **[The Transference](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-transference):** the Servant can still draw from the Master, as long as the Master has more than 1 card.
- **[The Wrath](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-wrath):** if the Servant draws, the Servant dies. If the Master draws, the Master dies, and the Servant is left in The Drought.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays Empowering: P2 is the Master, P3 is the Servant.
2. P3 has to draw 2. They take the top 2 cards of P2's hand.
3. P2 draws a card. It goes to the back of their hand.
</Callout>
