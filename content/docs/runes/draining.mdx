---
title: Draining
description: Steal 4 cards from another player, blind.
---

## Effect

Choose another living player, and take 4 cards from their hand, without seeing their faces. It doesn't count as drawing. The choice follows the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

Draining can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## The Rite

<Rite
  play="Draining is played, and a player is chosen."
  effect="You take 4 cards from the chosen player's hand."
/>

## Tainted

Take 8 cards.

## Transmutation

Take the chosen player's whole hand.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** take the whole hands of two players.

## Deathmatches

- **[The Drought](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-drought)** and **[The Transference](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-transference):** you don't die.
- **[The Wrath](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-wrath):** you die, and the player you stole from doesn't. An **Impermanence** or a **Shattered Impermanence** can save you, but only by cancelling your own steal.

## Exceptions

- **Miracle** can't be stolen.
- If you take a player's last cards, they die.
- The card pledged under a player's [dagger](../rules/sacrifice.mdx) isn't in their hand, so it can't be stolen.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays Draining and points at P3, who has 6 cards.
2. P1 takes 4 of them, without looking. P3 has 2 cards left.
</Callout>
