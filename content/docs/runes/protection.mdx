title: Protection
description: Send a Chain back to the player who started it.
---

## Effect

Protection can be played in two ways:

- **On a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx):** the player who started the Chain draws all of it.
- **On its own:** it works as a Choose Color.

## The Rite

<Rite
  play="Protection is played on a Chain."

/>

## Tainted

The player who started the Chain draws double.

## Transmutation

Play Protection together with Transmutation, and you receive a **Shattered Impermanence**. It protects only you: use it to block one thing aimed at you. You can use it out of turn, whenever you need it.

A Shattered Impermanence can't be used against **Impermanence**.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** you receive two Shattered Impermanences.

## Exceptions

- **On a Chain you started:** the Chain is cancelled, and that counts as your turn.
- **On a Protection:** if someone uses Protection to send a Chain to you, you can play your own Protection to send it back to them.
- **Not on a [Fortune](./fortune.mdx):** Protection can't block Fortune, but if a Protection sends a Chain to you, you can block it with Fortune.

## Examples

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays a +2. P2 plays a +4. The Chain is at 6.
2. P3 plays Protection. P1 started the Chain, so P1 draws 6.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 starts a Chain, and it grows to 8.
2. P3 plays Protection, sending it to P1.
3. P1 plays Protection too, sending it back. P3 draws 8.
</Callout>
