---
title: Vision
description: Make a player show their hand to everyone.
---

## Effect

Choose a player. They show their hand to everyone, right when Vision is played. It's shown once; nothing lasts.

You have to choose someone, and they must be alive. You can choose yourself, though it rarely helps. The choice follows the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

Vision can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## The Rite

<Rite
  play="Vision is played, and a player is chosen."
  effect="The chosen player shows their hand to everyone, once."
/>

## Tainted

Choose two players.

## Transmutation

The chosen player's hand stays shown until they die or the game ends.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** two players' hands stay shown until they die or the game ends.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays Vision and points at P3.
2. P3 shows their hand to the whole table, then takes it back.
</Callout>
