---
title: Prosperity
description: Doubles a + Chain, or the next one.
---

## Effect

Prosperity always multiplies a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx) by 2. It can't be played without its effect.

- **On a Chain:** the Chain is doubled, and passes to the next player, who continues it or draws.
- **On its own:** the next Chain is doubled when it ends. Played this way, you also choose a colour, like a Choose Color.

It can be played into a Chain.

## The Rite

<Rite
  play="Prosperity is played, on a Chain or on its own (choosing a colour)."
  happening="The current Chain is doubled, or the next one will be."
/>

## Tainted

The multiplier becomes ×4.

## Transmutation

Every Chain is doubled for the rest of the game.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** every Chain is multiplied by 6 for the rest of the game.

## Examples

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays a +2. P2 plays a +4. The Chain is at 6.
2. P3 plays Prosperity. The Chain is at 12, and passes to P4.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays Prosperity on a green 5, and chooses blue.
2. Later, P3 starts a Chain with a +2, and P4 adds a +4. The Chain is at 6.
3. P5 can't continue it. The Chain ends and is doubled: P5 draws 12.
</Callout>
