## Effect

Choose any 3 cards from the [graveyard](../rules/piles.mdx#graveyard), and add them to your hand. The graveyard is face up, so you can pick exactly the cards you want.

Taking cards from the graveyard counts as drawing: a [Starved](./famine.mdx) player who plays Desecration dies.

Desecration can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## Dirt

If the graveyard is empty, you get a **Dirt** card instead, into your hand. Dirt is played like any card, on its own turn, so you can hold it for the right moment. When you play it, choose a player and throw dirt in their face: they can't play purple cards for 3 turns.

## Tainted

Take 6 cards.

## Transmutation

Take 7 random cards from the graveyard, and summon them as an **extra hand**, in a new seat anywhere you choose at the table. You imbued your soul into it: you're its master, and you play it on its turns. It has its own [dagger](../rules/sacrifice.mdx).

If either of your two hands wins, you win. If one of them dies, the other keeps playing.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** take 14 cards, and summon two extra hands of 7, each in a seat you choose.

## Deathmatches

- **[The Drought](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-drought)** and **[The Transference](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-transference):** you don't die.
- **[The Wrath](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-wrath):** you die.
