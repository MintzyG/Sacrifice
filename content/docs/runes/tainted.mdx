## How it works

Tainted is a purple card, but you can't play it on its own. While it's in your hand, you're **Tainted**.

- **Doubled plays:** many of your plays are stronger. Every rune page has a **Tainted** section saying how.
- **Basic cards:** your + cards are doubled, your Blocks block two players, and your Reverses don't reverse.
- **Deathmatches:** in any [Deathmatch](../rules/deathmatches.mdx), you can choose not to draw.

## Winning

Tainted can't be played on its own, and it can't be pledged, so you can't win by [Sacrifice](../rules/sacrifice.mdx). The only way to win while Tainted is to be the last player alive. If only you and one other player are left, they win.

## Getting rid of it

- **Dying.** If you're killed by **Reaper**, Tainted goes to the player who killed you.
- **[Time](./time.mdx)**, but only its Transmutation, which restarts the game.
- **[Pandemonium](./pandemonium.mdx)**, which shuffles it in with every other card.
- **Becoming Impotent:** Tainted becomes a common purple wild card.
- **Becoming pure, with Purity:** the taint is removed, and Tainted becomes a common purple wild card.
- **Being Cleansed:** if you have both **Miracle** and Tainted, you can discard both, and come back with a new hand: 7 cards, or 1 during any Deathmatch.
- **Impermanence:** play it with Tainted to destroy Tainted. The Impermanence is used up too.
- **[Draining](./draining.mdx):** someone steals it from you.
- **[Your Servant](./empowering.mdx):** they draw it from your hand.
- **Transmutation:** play it with Tainted to start **Pestilence** (see below).

## Where it stays

Tainted stays in your hand when hands move:

- **[Soul Swap](./soul-swap.mdx):** every card is swapped except Tainted.
- **[Transference](./transference.mdx):** your hand passes on, but Tainted stays with you.

## Transmutation

Play Tainted with Transmutation, and **Pestilence** begins: Tainted leaves your hand, and for the rest of the game, every player gets the Tainted effects. Everyone can win.
