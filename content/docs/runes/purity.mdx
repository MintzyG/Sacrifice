---
title: Purity
description: Cleanse a player of every bad effect.
---

## Effect

Choose one player, yourself included. Every bad effect on them is removed: being Starved, Impotent, blocked by Stasis, a Servant or a Scapegoat, cursed, [Indebted](../rules/greed.mdx#not-enough-runes), and so on. The choice follows the [Choice Rule](../rules/choice-rule.mdx).

- **[Tainted](./tainted.mdx):** the taint is removed, and their Tainted becomes a common purple wild card.
- **Only what's already there:** new bad effects can still be put on them afterwards.
- **Even the powerless:** an [Impotent](./impotence.mdx) player can still use Purity. Purity's power is far greater than anything they've lost.

Purity can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## Tainted

Choose two players.

## Transmutation

**Purified Soul:** cleanse the chosen player, and from then on, no bad effect can be put on them.

A Purified Soul stays on the hand, like any effect (see [Effect Transfer](../rules/effect-transfer.mdx)). But it's a risk: if a Purified Soul dies, it can't be revived in any way, not even by [Miracle](./miracle.mdx). With a [Reaper](./reaper.mdx) still in the game, that matters.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** two Purified Souls.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
P2 is Impotent and Starved, and is P4's Servant.

1. P2 plays Purity on themselves. It still works, even though they're Impotent.
2. P2 is no longer Impotent, Starved, or a Servant.
</Callout>
