---
title: Time
description: Undo the last 4 turns.
---

## Effect

The game goes back 4 [turns](../rules/turns.mdx). Everything that happened in [The Rite](../rules/the-rite.mdx) during those turns is undone: the played cards go back to whoever played them, and every happening, event, effect and death is reversed. Then play continues from the player after you.

Time can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx), only before or after one.

## The Rite

<Rite
  play="Time is played."
  event="The last 4 turns are undone."
/>

## Reversed deaths

A player whose death is undone comes back with a new hand, not their old one: 7 cards, or 1 card during any [Deathmatch](../rules/deathmatches.mdx). The cards come from the draw pile, or, if there's no draw pile left, at random from the used pile. Their old hand stays where it was.

Getting cards back from Time never kills anyone, even during a Deathmatch.

## Tainted

The game goes back 8 turns.

## Transmutation

The game restarts. The cards already played stay on the used pile. Every player's hand and the draw pile are put back together, then dealt out again: 7 cards each, one at a time around the table. If there aren't enough cards, deal until they run out. The first to play is whoever was next after the last card played.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** the game restarts, the same way.

## Exceptions

- **Early in the game:** if Time is played within the first 4 turns of the game, the game restarts.
- **[Hail Mary](../rules/hail-mary.mdx):** Time can be played as your Hail Mary, undoing the previous player's win.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays a red 3.
2. P2 plays Reaper on P4, killing them.
3. P3 plays a red 7.
4. P4 is dead, so play moves on: P1 plays a red Block, and P2 is blocked.
5. P3 plays Time. The last 4 turns are undone: the red Block, the red 7 and Reaper go back to their players, P2 is no longer blocked, and P4 comes back with 7 new cards. Play continues from P4.
</Callout>
