## Effect

Choose 3 cards from your own hand, and discard them. They go to the [graveyard](../rules/piles.mdx#graveyard).

- If you have 3 cards or fewer left, crushing them kills you.
- You can crush [Tainted](./tainted.mdx), and be rid of it.
- You can't crush [Miracle](./miracle.mdx).

If Crushing is your last card, it works as a Choose Color.

Crushing can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## Tainted

Discard 6 cards. With 6 or fewer left, you die.

## Transmutation

Destroy any 1 card in the game, wherever it is: in anyone's hand, or in any pile. This includes Miracle.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** destroy any 2 cards.
