## Effect

Take the last purple card on the [used pile](../rules/piles.mdx#used-pile) into your hand. Any purple card counts: runes, and purple +2, +4, Reverse, Block and Choose Color.

Healing can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## The Rite

<Rite
  play="Healing is played."
  effect="You take the last purple card from the used pile into your hand."
/>

## Void Reach

If there's no purple card on the used pile, you get **Void Reach** instead: a copy of any card in the game, including runes. It's a card in your hand like any other, just a copy.

## Tainted

Take the last two purple cards.

## Transmutation

Take any purple card of your choice from the used pile.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** take any two purple cards of your choice.

## Deathmatches

- **[The Drought](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-drought)** and **[The Transference](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-transference):** you don't die.
- **[The Wrath](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-wrath):** you die when you play it. But if the card you take is an **Impermanence**, you can use it to cancel your own death. A **Shattered Impermanence** can save you too.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
1. P1 plays Stasis on P2.
2. A few turns later, P3 plays Healing. Stasis is the last purple card on the used pile, so P3 takes it into their hand.
</Callout>
