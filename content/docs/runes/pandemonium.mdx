---
title: Pandemonium
description: Shuffle every hand together and deal them out again.
---

## Effect

Every player's hand goes into one pile, which is shuffled. Then the player who played Pandemonium deals it out, one card at a time, starting with the next player in turn order, until the pile runs out.

- **Dead players are dealt in too.** They come back alive with the cards they get.
- **Every effect is removed**, from every hand: roles like Master, Servant, Starved, Scapegoat and Sinner, blocks from Stasis, and so on (see [Effect Transfer](../rules/effect-transfer.mdx)).
- **Miracle** isn't shuffled in. It stays with its owner.
- **Tainted** is shuffled in, like any other card.

Pandemonium can't be played into a [Chain](../rules/chains.mdx).

## Tainted

No change.

## Transmutation

The hands swap places with the draw pile: every player's hand becomes the new draw pile, and the old draw pile is dealt out as the new hands, one card at a time until it runs out.

**Tainted and Transmutation together:** the same.

## Deathmatches

- **[The Drought](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-drought):** nothing special. But the Transmutation version kills everyone.
- **[The Transference](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-transference):** everyone dies except Starved players, who get the cards.
- **[The Wrath](../rules/deathmatches.mdx#the-wrath):** everyone dies.

A player who dies from Pandemonium isn't dealt any cards.

## Exceptions

- **[Time](./time.mdx):** if Time undoes Pandemonium, the hands are shuffled and dealt out again.

## Example

<Callout title="Example">
P1 has 2 cards and is Starved. P2 has 9, including Pandemonium. P3 is dead.

1. P2 plays Pandemonium.
2. P2 has 8 cards left. The 10 cards are shuffled, and P2 deals them starting with P3: P3, P1, P2, P3, P1, P2…
3. P3 is alive again. P1 is no longer Starved. Everyone has 3 or 4 cards.
</Callout>
